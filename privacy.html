Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy explains what KarmaKit (karmakitapp.com) collects, why, and what you can do about it. It describes the service as actually built — if you change how the app works, update this page too.

Who we are

KarmaKit is operated by Brady Ali Medina, contact hello@karmakitapp.com. For privacy questions, use that address.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, and your display name. If you register with email and password, the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google — nothing else.
  • Profile details you choose to add. Company or school, and a short bio. All optional.
  • Your activity. Projects you submit, votes you cast, feedback you write, and connection requests you send. Each is stored with your account ID and a timestamp.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

Who can see what

  • Public to signed-in users: your display name, your company if you set one, the projects you submit, and the feedback you write.
  • Private by default: your email address. It is shared with another user only when you send them a connection request, which puts your email in front of that specific builder deliberately.
  • Visible to other signed-in users: the votes you cast, including which projects you passed on. We plan to restrict this; until then, treat votes as non-private.

Where your data lives

KarmaKit runs on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers and is subject to Google's security practices. Depending on the region selected for the database, data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, your projects, your votes, your feedback, and connection requests you sent. Backups held by Google may persist for a short period afterwards.

Your rights

From your profile page you can, at any time and without contacting us:

  • Export everything we hold about you as a JSON file.
  • Correct your display name, company and bio.
  • Delete your account and associated content permanently.

If you are in the UK, EU, or another region with data protection law, these cover your rights of access, rectification and erasure. To object to processing or raise a complaint, email us at the address above.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage for one purpose only: keeping you signed in between visits. This is set by Firebase Authentication and is required for the service to work. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Children

KarmaKit is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how your data is used, notify you by email.

This document is a starting point written to match what the application does. It is not legal advice. Have it reviewed before launching commercially, particularly if you take payments or operate in the EU or UK.
